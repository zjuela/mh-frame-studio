> This is a plain-language starting point. Have it reviewed for the countries you operate in (for example HIPAA in the US, GDPR in the EU/UK) before taking on client work.

## What we collect

- **Contact details** you send us through the website or by email: name, email address, business name and your message.
- **Client files** you send for editing: photos, videos and any notes.

## How we use it

- To reply to you and deliver the work you ordered.
- We never sell your data and never use client files for anything other than your project.
- We never show client work in our portfolio without written permission.

## Patient images

- Patient images are transferred through encrypted links and accessed only by the editor assigned to your account.
- Files are deleted **30 days after delivery** unless you ask us to keep them longer.
- You are responsible for obtaining patient consent to share and publish images.
- On request we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) or Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Contact us at hello@brightframe.studio.
