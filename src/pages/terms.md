## Orders and delivery

- Turnaround times start when we receive all files and instructions.
- Every order includes two rounds of revisions. Further rounds or new requests are quoted separately.

## Plans and payment

- Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month and can be cancelled any time before the next billing date.
- Unused photo edits roll over for one month.

## Ownership

- You keep all rights to your original files and to the edited files we deliver once paid.
- You confirm you have the right, including patient consent, to share the files you send us.

## Editing policy

We correct photography (light, colour, framing, background). We do not alter clinical results such as tooth shape, shade or gum line.
